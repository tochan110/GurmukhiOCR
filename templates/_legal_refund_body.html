This Refund Policy applies to paid purchases for GurmukhiOCR OCR credit packs and related paid features sold through our website and payment processors.

What you are buying

Paid plans are one-time purchases of OCR credits (also called pages/images). Each credit generally covers OCR for one PDF page or one image. Credits are digital goods delivered to your account immediately after successful payment confirmation.

Satisfaction / refund window

If you are not satisfied with a paid credit pack purchase, you may request a refund within {{ business.refund_days }} days of the purchase date by emailing contact email from the email address on your GurmukhiOCR account.

Please include:

  • Your account email / username
  • Purchase date and order / receipt ID from the payment provider
  • A brief reason for the refund request

When refunds are granted

We generally grant refunds when:

  • You request a refund within {{ business.refund_days }} days of purchase, and
  • You have used only a small portion of the purchased credits (for example, fewer than {{ business.refund_max_credits_used }} credits), or
  • There was a clear billing error (duplicate charge, wrong amount), or
  • Paid credits were not delivered to your account after a successful charge

When refunds may be denied

We may decline a refund when:

  • The {{ business.refund_days }}-day window has passed
  • A substantial portion of the purchased credits has already been consumed
  • The request appears abusive, fraudulent, or violates our Terms of Service
  • The purchase was free monthly allowance credits (these are not paid products)

How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through our payment processor. Processing times depend on the processor and your bank or card issuer (often several business days). When a refund is approved for a credit pack, we may remove the corresponding unused paid credits from your account.

Free credits

Monthly free credits are complimentary and are not refundable cash purchases. Unused free credits reset according to your account’s monthly cycle.

Chargebacks

If you have a billing problem, contact us first so we can help. Filing a chargeback without contacting support may delay resolution and can result in account suspension while the dispute is investigated.
